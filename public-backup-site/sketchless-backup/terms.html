Private purpose

Sketchless Private Backups is a personal, owner-operated utility maintained by @kevincyril. It copies authorized Sketchless application data and CAD artifacts to the owner's Google Drive. These public pages describe the tool; visiting them does not create an account, grant access to backups, or enroll anyone in a backup service.

Authorized access

Only operate the tool with the account owner's authorization and credentials. The operator must have the right to process and back up the source records, including any saved user conversations and designs. Do not use it to access another person's data without permission, bypass provider restrictions or publish private information. Backup processing and retention are described in the privacy policy.

Owner controls and responsibilities

The owner controls the Google OAuth grant, private Drive folder, repository secrets and workflow settings. Keep those credentials private, review access periodically, and revoke them when no longer needed. The owner is responsible for monitoring failed runs, maintaining available storage and free-plan allowances, and testing recovery. Revocation does not erase existing copies; removing data requires the deletion steps in the privacy policy.

Retention permanently deletes eligible older verified backup-app archives after a new backup has passed verification, keeping eight. An archive is a snapshot: it may retain records later changed or removed from the source. Review retention and existing copies when handling a deletion request.

Availability and costs

The setup targets existing free hosting, runner and storage allowances. This site charges no subscription fee and collects no payment information. GitHub, Google and Neon set their own quotas, terms and prices. Free-plan capacity and scheduled execution are not guaranteed; jobs can be delayed, interrupted or blocked. Do not upgrade plans, attach billing or enable paid overages as part of this setup.

Technical limits

The tool checks archive integrity, a local restore and the uploaded file's checksum. These checks do not guarantee future availability, complete disaster recovery or restoration of browser access without the original ownership cookies. Backups do not include runtime secrets or resume in-flight generation. A successful data backup does not establish that a generated CAD design is accurate, safe or suitable for manufacturing.

The utility is provided as available, without a service-level commitment or a promise that every loss can be recovered. Keep any additional copies you need and do not rely on this tool as the only protection for irreplaceable information. Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Changes, stopping use and contact

The owner can disable the schedule or revoke authorization at any time. The tool or these terms may change as the implementation changes; updated terms will show a new effective date. For help or questions, use the support email shown on the tool's Google authorization screen. The maintainer's public identity is @kevincyril. Never post tokens, credentials, backups or private design content in public support messages.
